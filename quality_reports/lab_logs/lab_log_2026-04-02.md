## 2. Fact-Check Findings

### Finding 1: CRITICAL — `fuel_cost3` is $/MWh, Not $/MMBTU

**Evidence:** `gtep/gtep_model.py:1889-1891`:
```python
m.fuelCost3 = Param(
    m.thermalGenerators, initialize=fuelCost3, units=u.USD / (u.MW * u.hr)
)
```
The units `USD / (MW * hr)` = `$/MWh`. This is the **total marginal cost** at the 2035 fuel price projection, not a fuel price per unit of heat content.

**The Bug:** Cell 3 of the notebook did:
```python
gen_df.loc[~is_renewable, "Fuel Price $/MMBTU"] = gen_df.loc[~is_renewable, "fuel_cost3"]
```
This places a $/MWh value directly into the "Fuel Price $/MMBTU" column. Prescient then computes:
```
MC = Fuel_Price ($/MMBTU) × HR_incr_1 (BTU/kWh) × 0.001
```
Creating a double-counted cost for generators with HR ≠ 1000 BTU/kWh.

**Impact by fuel type:**

| Generator | Type | Fuel Price (wrong) | HR_incr_1 | MC (wrong) | fuel_cost3 (correct MC) | Error Factor |
|-----------|------|--------------------|-----------|------------|-------------------------|-------------|
| Gen 2 | CT | $22.80 | 927.39 | $21.15 | $22.80 | 0.93× |
| Gen 26 | COAL | $18.94 | 8910.96 | $168.77 | $18.94 | 8.9× |
| Gen 1 | NUC | $7.38 | 16453.39 | $121.40 | $7.38 | 16.5× |

CT generators appeared reasonable by coincidence (HR_incr ≈ 1000, so the ×0.001 factor ≈ 1.0). COAL and NUC costs were inflated by 9–16×, which would have produced nonsensical dispatch: coal and nuclear would never dispatch, making CT the sole baseload, yielding wildly wrong LMPs.

**Fix:** Back-calculate the correct Fuel Price per generator:
```python
Fuel_Price = fuel_cost3 / (HR_incr_1 × 0.001)
```
This ensures `MC = Fuel_Price × HR_incr_1 × 0.001 = fuel_cost3` exactly.

**Verification:**
```
CT Gen 2:    FP=24.587 × HR=927.39 × 0.001 = $22.80/MWh  ✓
COAL Gen 26: FP=2.125  × HR=8911.0 × 0.001 = $18.94/MWh  ✓
NUC Gen 1:   FP=0.448  × HR=16453  × 0.001 = $7.38/MWh   ✓
```

Back-calculated fuel prices are physically reasonable:
- NUC: ~$0.39–0.45/MMBTU (nuclear fuel is cheap, heat rates are high)
- COAL: ~$2.13–4.98/MMBTU (typical coal price range)
- CT: ~$16.76–27.46/MMBTU (gas price varies per gen due to different HR_incr_1 values)

## 4. Lessons Learned

- `[LEARN:fuel_cost3]` `fuel_cost3` in GTEP model is `USD/(MW·hr)` = **$/MWh** (total marginal cost), NOT $/MMBTU. To convert to Prescient's `Fuel Price $/MMBTU`, must divide by `HR_incr_1 × 0.001`.
- `[LEARN:validation]` Cost sanity checks should compare MC against expected $/MWh ranges per fuel type (CT: $15–30, COAL: $15–25, NUC: $5–10). The original sanity check only printed values without flagging the 9–16× error.
- `[LEARN:coincidence]` CT generators masked the bug because `HR_incr_1 ≈ 1000 BTU/kWh`, making `× 0.001 ≈ 1.0`. Always check ALL fuel types, not just gas CTs.
