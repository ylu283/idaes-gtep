## Domain Review & Critical Bug Fix

A domain review identified **2 CRITICAL** issues in the initial implementation:

1. **CRITICAL: Cost curve double-counting** — Initial version used `Prescient/gen.csv` as base, which encodes `Fuel Price=1, HR_incr=fuel_cost`. Setting Fuel Price to fuel_cost3 created `fuel_cost²` marginal costs (e.g., CT Gen 2: $0.52/MWh instead of correct $21.15/MWh).
2. **CRITICAL: Wrong source file** — `Prescient/gen.csv` is jkskolf's intermediate with 2-segment curves, no ramp rates, and broken cost encoding. `Prescient_2/gen.csv` is the correct source.
3. **MAJOR: Output_pct_0=0.6 for renewables** — Candidate templates set 60% minimum output for PV/WIND. Fixed to 0.0.

**All issues fixed.** See `quality_reports/reports/2026-04-01_gtep_2035_conversion_domain_review.md` for full details.

## Known Issues & Caveats

1. **Synthesized generators**: Some generators were created from templates because they exist in GTEP solution and timeseries but not in any gen.csv. Their non-cost parameters (reactive power, ramp rates) are approximate.
2. **Candidate CT cost curves**: Use median HR from existing CTs in Prescient_2/gen.csv. Actual new-build CT heat rates may differ.

## Update (2026-04-02): Fuel Price Units Bug Fix

A fact-check revealed that `fuel_cost3` is in $/MWh (not $/MMBTU). The previous version placed it directly in
the "Fuel Price $/MMBTU" column, causing Prescient to double-count via `FP × HR × 0.001`. This inflated COAL
costs by ~9× and NUC costs by ~16×. CTs appeared correct by coincidence (HR ≈ 1000).

**Fix:** Back-calculate `Fuel Price = fuel_cost3 / (HR_incr_1 × 0.001)` per generator.
See `quality_reports/lab_logs/lab_log_2026-04-02.md` for full details.
